## Mandatory Reading Material
1. WordPiece and BPE tokenizer papers
2. [SentencePiece](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/SentencePiece%3A-A-simple-and-language-independent-Kudo-Richardson/b5246fa284f86b544a7c31f050b3bd0defd053fd): A simple and language independent subword tokenizer and detokenizer for Neural Text Processing
4. [A study of smoothing methods for language models applied to information retrieval](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/984321.984322)
5. [C.J.C. Burges, From RankNet to LambdaRank to LambdaMART: An Overview. Microsoft Research Technical Report MSR-TR-2010-82, 2010](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B6x5IP0EWft1V0txX2FXbmM0dWc/view?resourcekey=0-ODR5FOR3PjY5B0ZA6JKNYQ)
6. [C.J.C. Burges, R. Ragno and Q.V. Le, Learning to Rank with Non-Smooth Cost Functions. NIPS 2006](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B6x5IP0EWft1NlE5emR5ZC1GZlk/view?resourcekey=0-7jEPWdCxSgwC4x5xnQmcRQ)
7. [ColBERT: Efficient and Effective Passage Search via Contextualized Late Interaction over BERT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12832)
8. [CITADEL: Conditional Token Interaction via Dynamic Lexical Routing for Efficient and Effective Multi-Vector Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.10411)
   

## Recommended Reading Material
1. Survey on Open-Vocabulary Modeling and Tokenization in NLP ([Mielke et al.](https://www.semanticscholar.org/reader/d617f51833860dc50d202af7f80be71304b2e994))
2. [The Probabilistic Relevance Framework: BM25 and Beyond](http://privateweb.iitd.ac.in/~srikanta/2026-col7364/foundations_bm25_review.pdf)
3. [A study of Poisson query generation model for information retrieval](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1277741.1277797)
4. [Efficient Estimation of Word Representations in Vector Space](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.3781) (Aka the Word2Vec paper)
5. [Neural Word Embedding as Implicit Matrix Factorization](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2014/file/b78666971ceae55a8e87efb7cbfd9ad4-Paper.pdf)
6. [Dependency-Based Word Embeddings](https://aclanthology.org/P14-2050.pdf)
7. [A Dual Embedding Space Model for Document Ranking](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.01137) (Extended version of the WWW'16 paper)
8. [BM25 Wins at Scale: A Scaling Study of Retrieval-Augmented Generation Paradigms](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2607.26497)
